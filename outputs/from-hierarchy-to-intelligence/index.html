What the company builds

01 Capabilities

Reusable financial building blocks: payments, lending, banking, and more. Each has reliability, compliance, and performance targets.

02 World model

Two kinds of context: recorded company work and operations; customer and merchant transaction patterns. The model is intended to develop predictive and causal understanding.

03 Intelligence

Use that context to combine capabilities into timely solutions. When a needed capability is missing, the gap informs the backlog.

04 Interfaces

Hardware and software surfaces, including Square and Cash App, deliver the composed solutions.
